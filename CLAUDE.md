# Otian AI Consulting: how to work here

One static page selling Jack and Jett's consulting. Same family as otianai.com (its colors, Georgia
over Inter, the Otian mark) and a different layout on purpose: that site sells software and ends on
a download, this one sells two people and ends on a booked call (Jett, 2026-10-08: "same family, but
not completely the same").

- **otianai.com's rules govern the words here.** The house style in its CLAUDE.md ("Sounds like a
  person": no antithesis, no machine words, 35 words a paragraph, buttons are a verb and an outcome,
  no em dashes, American example names). Any claim about Archie must be in its TRUST.md, and any
  money figure in its FACTS.md, exactly as if this page lived there. The repo is
  `/Users/Games/Desktop/Code/Otian-AI`.
- **Quotes are word for word** from otianai.com/testimonials/. Never trim inside the quote marks.
- **Never name or judge another company.** The difference is shown, two named people who answer,
  never argued.
- **The colors are copied from otianai.com's `css/styles.css`** (`:root` and `[data-theme="dark"]`).
  If the brand moves there, move it here. Dark mode follows the system, with no script.
- **Bump `?v=` on the stylesheet link when `css/site.css` changes**, for the same CDN cache reason
  otianai.com does.
- **Commits:** lowercase, no Claude attribution, and never push; Jett pushes.
